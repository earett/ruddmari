CLIENT BRIEF
Mari's Smash Burgers · Food truck, Oak Cliff

"My truck is losing money. Help me turn it around."

Dear Consultants,

My family has run this burger truck for three years. People line up for my double smash burger at lunch. But since my helper's pay and the commissary fee went up, I lose money every month.

Last month I sold 1,250 burgers at $8.00 each. The bun, beef, toppings, and wrapper for one burger cost me $3.00. My fixed bills are $7,500 a month no matter how many burgers I sell.

I can only handle two changes at a time and spend no more than $1,000 up front. And breaking even isn't enough: I need to clear at least $250 a month to save for new tires. What should I do?

— Marisol
